## Description
S1Print prints PDFs and images from your iPhone or iPad to the Luck Jingle S1 pocket thermal printer over Bluetooth.

• Share from any app: Safari, Files, Mail or your invoicing app. Pick S1Print in the share sheet and print.
• See exactly what prints: a true black-and-white preview of the 384-dot raster sent to the printer.
• Made for 58 mm paper: content is fitted to the 48 mm printhead, with light, medium and dark printing and label paper.
• Reliable over Bluetooth Low Energy: S1Print follows the printer's own flow control, so long receipts print without gaps, and it tells you when the printer is out of paper, open, too hot or low on battery.
• Private: no accounts, no tracking and no internet connection. Your documents go only to your printer.
• Free and open source.

Made by the team behind Facta DTE, electronic invoicing for El Salvador.

Compatible with the Luck Jingle S1 (PPS1). Other Luck Jingle models are untested. S1Print is not affiliated with Luck Jingle or Xiamen Lujiang Technology.

## Review notes
S1Print needs a Luck Jingle S1 Bluetooth thermal printer, which App Review is unlikely to have.
A video of the full flow on a real iPhone with the printer is attached: open a PDF from Safari's share sheet, select the printer, preview and print.
Without a printer you can still import a PDF or image (Documents → Import), see the monochrome preview and adjust print options; the Print button needs a printer nearby.
No account or login is required.
