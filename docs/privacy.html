S1Print Privacy Policy

Applies to S1Print for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android. Last updated 23 October 2026.

In short: S1Print does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Documents you print. PDFs and images you open or share with S1Print are converted on your device and sent only to your printer over Bluetooth. Copies of shared documents are kept on your device so you can print them again, and you can delete them in the app.
  • Bluetooth. S1Print uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to your thermal printer. It remembers the printer you chose, on your device.
  • Settings. Print options such as darkness and paper size are saved on your device.
  • Diagnostics. The iOS app keeps a short Bluetooth log on your device to help fix printing problems. It is never sent anywhere; you decide whether to share it with us.

What we never do

We do not send your documents, settings or any identifier to us or to third parties, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Links in the app (source code, this page, factadte.com) open in your browser, which follows its own privacy terms.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub. S1Print is developed by Marvin Quevedo and the team behind Facta DTE.

En español

S1Print no recopila datos personales. Los documentos que imprime se procesan en su dispositivo y solo se envían a su impresora por Bluetooth. La impresora elegida, los ajustes y el registro de diagnóstico se guardan en su dispositivo y nunca se envían a nadie. No hay cuentas, publicidad ni analítica.
